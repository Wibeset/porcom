The best Sequel alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sequel is a polished app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro that keeps movies, series, books, audiobooks and video games in one library. People look for an alternative when they only track what they watch and want an app built around that, when the features they need most, such as where to watch and release reminders, sit behind the Sequel+ subscription, or when they want an app in French. Here are the alternatives worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are described honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV show journal for iPhone, in English and French, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud; we never receive it. Series are tracked episode by episode, one season at a time, with the next episode waiting in a Home Screen widget, and movies go into a diary with the date, a verdict (👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!) and every rewatch.

Each title opens on its trailer and shows the Canadian streaming services where it’s available, at no cost. Titles you spot in a trailer go on your watchlist, and your hours on screen, streaks and verdicts add up to a year in review. Porcom tracks only movies and series: no books, no games.

Best for: a private, free journal for movies and series, with nothing to unlock and no account to manage.

2. Sofa Time

Sofa Time is a TV show and movie tracker for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision, available in English, French, Spanish and many other languages. It includes a streaming guide covering hundreds of services, release reminders, recommendations and social features such as comments and following other users. It can sync with Trakt and Simkl and import from TV Time and IMDb. It’s free, with a Premium subscription or a lifetime purchase. See our Sofa Time alternative page.

Best for: a native Apple app focused on movies and TV, with imports and a social side.

3. Trakt

Trakt is the long-running tracking service behind many apps, including Sequel, which can sync with it through Sequel+. It needs an online account and runs on the web, iOS, Apple TV and more, and it can log what you watch automatically from media servers like Plex and Jellyfin. The free plan has in-house ads and caps lists at 250 items; VIP costs US$59.99 a year. See our Trakt alternative page.

Best for: automatic tracking across many devices and media servers.

4. Simkl

Simkl tracks TV shows, anime and movies from an online account, on its website and in the free Simkl Lists app for iPhone. It shows airing schedules and episode progress, and imports from Trakt, AniList, MyAnimeList, IMDb and Netflix. Paid memberships add more features. See our Simkl alternative page.

Best for: anime fans who also follow series and movies.

5. Serializd

Serializd is a social network for TV lovers on iOS and Android: you review shows, follow friends and join discussions. It doesn’t track movies. It’s free, with optional in-app purchases. See our Serializd alternative page.

Best for: sharing your opinions on series with a community.

6. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the best-known social network for film, on iOS, Android and the web, with reviews, lists and followers. It doesn’t track TV series yet. It’s free with ads, and paid plans (Pro and Patron) remove them and add features. See our Letterboxd alternative page.

Best for: film lovers who want public reviews and a community.

When Sequel suits you better

Sequel is the better choice if you want one library for everything you follow: books, audiobooks and video games as well as movies and series. It also runs on iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro, imports your history from several services, and, with Sequel+, syncs with Trakt, reminds you of releases and finds media mentioned on a web page. Porcom does none of that: it’s an iPhone journal for movies and series only.

Switching from Sequel, or using both

Porcom has no importer, so you rebuild your journal by hand. Search each series or movie (the data comes from TMDB) or type a title in yourself, pick the season and the episode you’re on, and add what you’ve already seen as watched, with the date if you remember it. Before you leave Sequel, use its in-app data export so you keep a copy of your library.

Many people keep both: Sequel for books and games, Porcom for the movies and series they watch. Your Porcom journal stays yours either way. Export a Porcom backup file from Profile › Data › Export a backup to restore on another iPhone, or send your watched movies to Letterboxd as a CSV file from Profile › Data › Export as CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Is Porcom really free?

Yes. Porcom is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. Where to watch, statistics and widgets are included for everyone.

Can I import my Sequel library into Porcom?

No. Porcom doesn’t import files from other apps. You add titles by searching TMDB or typing them in, and mark what you’ve already seen as watched.

Does Porcom track books or video games?

No. Porcom only tracks movies and TV series. If you follow books or games too, you can keep Sequel or another app for them.

Is Porcom available on iPad, Mac or Android?

No. Porcom is an iPhone app. Your journal syncs through your private iCloud and can be moved to another iPhone with a backup file.
