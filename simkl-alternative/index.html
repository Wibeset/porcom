The best Simkl alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Simkl tracks movies, TV shows and anime across the web, phones and media centers, and many TV Time users moved there after TV Time shut down on July 15, 2026. But it needs an account, shows ads on the free plan, keeps free profiles public and puts exports behind PRO or VIP. If you want something simpler or more private, here are the alternatives worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what each does better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV journal for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, so there is no public profile to hide. Follow series episode by episode, keep a movie diary with dates, verdicts and rewatches, and fill a watchlist with titles you spotted in a trailer. Each title opens on its trailer and shows where it’s streaming in Canada, and your hours, streaks and levels add up to a year in review.

Best for: a private, ad-free journal of movies and series on iPhone, in English or French.

2. Trakt

Trakt is the closest match to Simkl’s automatic side: it syncs with Plex, Jellyfin and other services, works on iOS, Apple TV and the web, and powers many third-party apps. It needs an account; the free plan has in-house ads and limits lists to 250 items, and VIP costs US$59.99 a year. See our Trakt alternative page for details.

Best for: scrobbling what you watch on a media center, across many devices.

3. Sofa Time

Sofa Time is an iPhone and iPad app for TV shows and movies that syncs your watchlist and history with Trakt or Simkl. That makes it a way to keep a Simkl account with a different iPhone app. It’s free, with a Premium subscription or lifetime purchase, and its interface is available in 16 languages, including French and Spanish. See our Sofa Time alternative page.

Best for: a polished iPhone app on top of an existing Simkl or Trakt account.

4. Serializd

Serializd is a social network for TV shows, on iOS and Android, where you rate and review seasons and episodes and follow friends. It doesn’t track movies. It’s free, with optional in-app purchases. Our Serializd alternative page compares it with Porcom.

Best for: writing and reading reviews of TV seasons with friends.

5. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the social network for film lovers, on iOS, Android and the web, with reviews, lists and followers. It tracks movies only for now; TV support has been announced as in development. It’s free with ads, with Pro and Patron subscriptions. See our Letterboxd alternative page.

Best for: movie lovers who want a public diary and a community.

When Simkl suits you better

Simkl does things Porcom doesn’t try to do. Choose it if you watch a lot of anime and want MyAnimeList sync, if you want your history scrobbled automatically from Plex, Kodi or a browser, or if you use Android, Windows or a web browser. It also imports from more than 20 services, which matters if you’re moving years of history from TV Time or Trakt, and it has a community with public profiles and lists.

Switching from Simkl, or using both

Porcom has no importer, so you start fresh. Search each title (Porcom uses TMDB) or type it in by hand, and begin with what you’re watching now: a series is tracked one season at a time, so add the current season and pick up from your episode. Your older history can stay on Simkl; with PRO or VIP, you can download it as a CSV file to keep a copy.

Many people keep both: Simkl for automatic tracking and anime, Porcom as a private journal with verdicts, notes and trailers. Your Porcom data stays portable too: back it up to a file and restore it on another iPhone, or export your watched movies as a CSV file in Letterboxd’s import format from Profile › Data › Export as CSV.

Questions

Can I import my Simkl history into Porcom?

No. Porcom doesn’t import from other apps. Add titles by searching for them or typing them in by hand, starting with the shows you’re watching now.

Is Porcom really free?

Yes. Porcom is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

Does Porcom track anime?

Porcom has no anime-specific features or MyAnimeList sync. You can add any title found in its TMDB search, or type one in by hand, and track it season by season like any other series.

Is Porcom on Android or the web?

No. Porcom is only available for iPhone, on the App Store.
