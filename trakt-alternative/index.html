The best Trakt alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Trakt is the power user’s tracker: it syncs with Plex and Jellyfin, runs on almost every screen and imports history from other services. But it needs an account, the free plan caps your watchlist at 250 titles and shows in-house ads, and VIP now costs US$59.99 a year, double what it did before 2025. If you just want to remember what you watched, here are the apps worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV journal for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no subscription. You follow each series episode by episode, keep a movie diary with dates, verdicts and rewatches, and fill a watchlist that has no limit. Your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, every title opens on its trailer with the services streaming it in Canada, and your hours, streaks and year in review come free.

Best for: iPhone users who want a private, hand-kept journal without an account or a paywall.

2. Simkl

Simkl is the closest thing to Trakt: a tracker for TV shows, anime and movies on the web, iOS and Android, with a free plan and paid Pro and VIP tiers. It can import your Trakt history once for free, or keep importing it automatically with VIP. Porcom vs Simkl.

Best for: anime fans and anyone who wants Trakt-style syncing across devices.

3. Serializd

Serializd is a social app for TV only: you log, rate and review episodes, seasons and whole shows, and read what friends think. It’s free on iOS and Android, with optional in-app purchases. It doesn’t track movies. Porcom vs Serializd.

Best for: series fans who want reviews and a community around their shows.

4. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the social network for film: diary, reviews, lists and followers, on iOS, Android and the web. It’s free with ads, with Pro and Patron plans. TV series are announced but not available yet. Porcom vs Letterboxd.

Best for: movie lovers who want to share reviews and lists.

5. Sequel

Sequel is a polished iPhone and iPad tracker for movies, TV shows, books and games, with episode check-offs and where-to-stream info. It’s free to download, with a Sequel+ subscription that unlocks everything. Porcom vs Sequel.

Best for: people who want one app for everything they watch, read and play.

6. JustWatch

JustWatch is a free streaming guide on the web, iOS and Android: it shows where a title is available in your country and lets you keep a watchlist. It’s built for finding something to watch rather than keeping a history. Porcom vs JustWatch.

Best for: deciding where to stream tonight’s pick.

When Trakt suits you better

Choose Trakt if you want tracking to happen on its own. It scrobbles from Plex, Jellyfin and many media center apps, so episodes get marked as watched without a tap. It also runs on the web, Android and TV boxes, imports your history from IMDb, Letterboxd and TV Time, and has a community for comments and recommendations. Porcom does none of that: it’s an iPhone journal you keep by hand.

Moving from Trakt to Porcom

Porcom has no importer, so you rebuild your journal yourself, which goes faster than it sounds:

  1. Download Porcom and search for the series you’re watching now (titles come from TMDB, or type one in by hand).
  2. Pick the season, then the episode you’re on. Add the movies and seasons you’ve already seen as watched, with the date if you remember it.
  3. Rebuild your watchlist from your Trakt watchlist, with no 250-title ceiling this time.

Once you’re in, your journal stays yours: Profile › Data › Export a backup saves a Porcom backup file you can restore on another iPhone, and Profile › Data › Export as CSV sends your watched movies to a file in Letterboxd’s import format. Nothing stops you from keeping Trakt for automatic syncing and Porcom for your verdicts and notes.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Trakt alternative with no list limits?

Yes. Porcom is free on iPhone with no ads, no subscription and no limit on your watchlist or your journal. Trakt’s free plan caps the watchlist at 250 titles.

Can I import my Trakt history into Porcom?

No. Porcom doesn’t import files from other apps. You add titles by searching TMDB or typing them in, then mark the seasons, episodes and movies you’ve already seen.

Does Porcom sync with Plex or Jellyfin?

No. Porcom is a journal you keep by hand on your iPhone. If you want automatic scrobbling from a media server, Trakt or Simkl are better fits.

Is Porcom on Android or the web?

No, Porcom is only available for iPhone. Your journal is stored on the device and synced with your private iCloud.
