TV Time is gone. Porcom keeps track of your shows.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

TV Time shut down on July 15, 2026. Porcom is a free TV show and movie tracker for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no server holding your history.

Download on the App Store

What happened to TV Time

Whip Media closed TV Time on July 15, 2026: the apps left the App Store and Google Play, the website went offline, and personal data was deleted after that date. Whip Media said a free app was no longer sustainable, and that there wasn’t enough demand for a paid one.

Why former TV Time users choose Porcom

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first title. No profile, no password.
  • Private by default. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive it.
  • Movies and series, by episode. Each season is its own entry, with its poster, trailer, dates and verdict.
  • Every title opens on its trailer, with the services where it’s streaming in Canada.
  • Verdicts, not just stars. 👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!, plus optional ratings by theme.
  • Watch the habit grow. Hours on screen, streaks, a yearly goal, twelve levels and a monthly challenge.
  • Your journal stays yours. Back it up to a file, restore it on a new iPhone, or export your movies to Letterboxd.

A journal that doesn’t depend on our servers

Porcom has no account and no server of its own: your journal lives on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, and you can export a Porcom backup file at any time. What you log stays with you.

Start in Porcom

  1. Download Porcom and search for the series you’re watching.
  2. Pick the season, then the episode you’re on. Add the movies and seasons you’ve already seen as watched, with the date if you remember it.

Porcom doesn’t import other apps’ files yet: you rebuild your journal by searching, title by title.

Kept your TV Time export? Porcom can’t read it, but keep the file: it’s your history.

When another app may suit you better

Porcom is iPhone only, with no social feed, no friends to follow and no sync with Plex or other media servers. If you need Android, the web or a community, look at Trakt, which imports TV Time exports.
