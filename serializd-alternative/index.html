Why people choose Porcom

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first title. No profile, no password.
  • Private by default. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive it.
  • Movies and series, by episode. Each season is its own entry, with its poster, trailer, dates and verdict.
  • Every title opens on its trailer, with the services where it’s streaming in Canada.
  • Verdicts, not just stars. 👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!, plus optional ratings by theme.
  • Watch the habit grow. Hours on screen, streaks, a yearly goal, twelve levels and a monthly challenge.
  • Your journal stays yours. Back it up to a file, restore it on a new iPhone, or export your movies to Letterboxd.

When Serializd suits you better

Serializd is a social network for TV lovers. Choose it if you want to review shows in public, follow friends and join discussions, or use Android.

Start in Porcom

  1. Download Porcom and search for the series you’re watching.
  2. Pick the season, then the episode you’re on. Add the movies and seasons you’ve already seen as watched, with the date if you remember it.

Porcom doesn’t import other apps’ files yet: you rebuild your journal by searching, title by title.
