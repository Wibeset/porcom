The best Serializd alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Serializd is a well-liked social network for TV: you log episodes, rate seasons, write reviews and follow friends. People look elsewhere for concrete reasons. It tracks TV only, so movies need a second app. It needs an account and keeps your diary in an online profile. The free app shows ads. And some people simply want a quiet journal without a feed. Here are the apps worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV journal for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Series are tracked season by season, episode by episode, and movies sit in the same diary, with the date, a verdict (👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!) and every rewatch.

Your watchlist keeps titles spotted in a trailer, each title shows where it’s streaming in Canada, and your hours, streaks and levels add up to a year in review. The journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. There is no social feed, and the app is iPhone only, in English and French.

Best for: keeping movies and series together in a private journal on iPhone.

2. Trakt

Trakt tracks movies and shows, and can record what you watch automatically from Plex, Jellyfin and other media centers. It works on the web and in apps for iOS, Apple TV and more.

It needs an account. The free plan shows in-house ads and limits each list to 250 items; VIP removes ads for US$59.99 a year. See our Trakt alternative page for a closer comparison.

Best for: a watch history filled in automatically from your media server.

3. Simkl

Simkl tracks TV shows, movies and anime in one online account, on the web, iOS and Android. It can import watch history from other services, including a TV Time export.

The core tracking is free, with paid plans (PRO and VIP) that add extras such as an ad-free app. Our Simkl alternative page compares it with Porcom.

Best for: anime fans and anyone moving a large history from another service.

4. TVmaze

TVmaze is a community-maintained TV database with episode tracking and a detailed air-date calendar. It’s TV only and works in the browser; there is no official App Store app.

Tracking is free, and premium tiers remove ads and add extras such as calendar feeds. It also imports TV Time exports.

Best for: precise episode calendars and air dates, on any computer.

5. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the movie network Serializd is often compared to. It has the same mix of diary, ratings, reviews and followers, but for films; TV tracking is announced as in development.

It’s free with ads, with Pro at CA$23.49 and Patron at CA$60.99 a year, on iOS, Android and the web. See our Letterboxd alternative page.

Best for: pairing with Serializd if you want a social diary for movies.

When Serializd suits you better

Serializd is built for talking about TV. Choose it if you want to publish reviews, follow friends, join discussions on episodes and seasons, and see what the community is rating. It also runs on Android and the web, which Porcom doesn’t.

If a public profile is part of the fun, Serializd is the better fit. Porcom is a private journal: nobody sees it unless you share a title, your year or your watchlist as an image.

Switching, or using both

Porcom doesn’t import files from other apps, so you rebuild your journal by searching. Search for the series you’re watching (titles come from TMDB), pick the season and the episode you’re on, then add the seasons and movies you’ve already seen as watched, with the date if you remember it. You can also type a title in by hand.

Plenty of people keep both: Serializd for reviews and conversations, Porcom for a private record of everything, movies included. Your Porcom journal can always leave: Profile › Data › Export as CSV writes your watched movies in Letterboxd’s import format, and Profile › Data › Export a backup saves a Porcom backup file you can restore on another iPhone.

Questions

Is there a Serializd alternative that tracks movies too?

Yes. Porcom, Trakt and Simkl all track movies and TV shows. Porcom keeps both in a private journal on iPhone, with no account; Trakt and Simkl are online services with web and mobile apps.

Is Porcom free?

Yes. Porcom is free, with no ads, no subscription and no account.

Can I import my Serializd history into Porcom?

No. Porcom doesn’t import other apps’ files. You add titles by searching, then mark the seasons, episodes and movies you’ve already watched.

Is Porcom on Android?

No, Porcom is iPhone only. If you need Android, Simkl has an Android app, and Trakt and TVmaze work in a browser.
