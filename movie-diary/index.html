A movie diary for every film you watch

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Porcom keeps a diary of the movies you watch: the date, your verdict, your notes and every rewatch, on your iPhone. Free, with no account and no ads.

Download on the App Store

Every movie opens on its trailer

Search a title: the poster, summary, cast and trailer come from TMDB. Then give it a verdict, 👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!, and rate the script, acting, directing, visuals, soundtrack and ending if you want to go further.

The date you watched, every time

Saw it last night? Mark it as watched and the evening counts. Watch it again and Porcom records a new viewing, so rewatches have their place in your calendar.

Milestones for movie lovers

A three-hour movie, a double feature, a classic from before 1970, a horror movie on October 31: milestones to unlock, twelve levels and a challenge each month.

Share your verdict

Share a movie with your verdict as an image, square for a post or tall for a story.

Your year in movies

Your hours on screen and the titles you watched, by platform and by genre. At the end of the year, your year on screen: the busiest month, the longest movie and the director of the year.

Already on Letterboxd?

Porcom exports the movies you’ve watched to a CSV file in Letterboxd’s import format, with the date, your verdict and your notes.

Free and private

Porcom works without an account. Your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, which we can’t access.

Download Porcom on the App Store: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
