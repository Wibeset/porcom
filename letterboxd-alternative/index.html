The best Letterboxd alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Letterboxd is the place to talk about movies, and that is exactly why some people look elsewhere. It is a public social network that needs an account, the free plan shows ads, and it still doesn’t track TV series episode by episode: in July 2026 it said TV tracking was in development, without a date. If you want one private place for movies and series, here are the apps worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV journal for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. Series are tracked one season at a time, episode by episode, and movies go into a diary with the date, a verdict (👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!) and every rewatch. Each title opens on its trailer, with where it’s streaming in Canada, and your hours on screen add up to a year in review.

Best for: a private journal of movies and series on iPhone, without a social feed.

2. Serializd

Serializd is often described as “Letterboxd for TV”: you log, rate and review shows and seasons, and follow friends. It is free on iOS and Android. It doesn’t track movies, so many people use it alongside Letterboxd. Porcom vs Serializd.

Best for: the Letterboxd social experience, for TV series.

3. Trakt

Trakt tracks movies and shows in an online account and can sync automatically with media players like Plex and Jellyfin. The free plan has ads and limits on lists; the VIP subscription removes them. It works on the web, iPhone, Apple TV and more. Porcom vs Trakt.

Best for: automatic scrobbling from your media center.

4. Sequel

Sequel is a polished media tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac that keeps movies, shows, books and more in one place. Tracking is free, with an optional Sequel+ subscription for extra features. Porcom vs Sequel.

Best for: one tracker for movies, shows and books on Apple devices.

5. IMDb

IMDb is the largest movie and TV database, with a free app on iOS and Android and a website. With a free account you can keep a watchlist and rate titles, and IMDb is one of the formats Letterboxd can import. It is a reference more than a journal. Porcom vs IMDb.

Best for: looking up cast and credits, with a simple watchlist.

When Letterboxd suits you better

Letterboxd is the best home for film culture online. Choose it if you want to write public reviews, follow friends and critics, browse the community’s lists, rent films in its Video Store, or use Android, a web browser or Apple TV. Porcom has no social features at all, and it only runs on iPhone.

Switching, or using both

Porcom has no importer: you add titles by searching (data from TMDB) or by typing them in. Start with what you’re watching now and your watchlist, then add favourites as they come back to you.

Going the other way is easy. Porcom exports the movies you’ve watched to a CSV file in Letterboxd’s import format, with the date, your verdict and your notes: tap Profile › Data › Export as CSV, then import the file on Letterboxd. Many people do exactly that: series and private notes in Porcom, public reviews on Letterboxd. For your own safety net, Porcom also exports a backup file you can restore on another iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Does Letterboxd track TV shows?

Not episode by episode. Letterboxd lists some limited series and TV movies, and in July 2026 it said full TV tracking was in development, without a release date. Porcom tracks every series one season at a time, by episode.

Is there a free Letterboxd alternative without ads?

Yes. Porcom is free with no ads, no subscription and no account. Serializd is also free, but it covers TV only.

Can I import my Letterboxd history into Porcom?

No. Porcom has no importer; you add titles by searching or by typing them in. You can, however, export your Porcom movies to Letterboxd as a CSV file.

Is Porcom on Android or the web?

No. Porcom is an iPhone app only. If you need Android or a browser, Letterboxd, Serializd, Trakt and IMDb are available there.
