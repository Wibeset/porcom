A Letterboxd alternative that tracks series too

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Porcom is a free journal for iPhone that keeps movies and series, episode by episode, in one private place. No account, no ads, and your movies can go back to Letterboxd anytime.

Why movie lovers add Porcom

  • No account, no sign-up. Open the app and add your first title. No profile, no password.
  • Private by default. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive it.
  • Movies and series, by episode. Each season is its own entry, with its poster, trailer, dates and verdict.
  • Every title opens on its trailer, with the services where it’s streaming in Canada.
  • Verdicts, not just stars. 👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!, plus optional ratings by theme.
  • Watch the habit grow. Hours on screen, streaks, a yearly goal, twelve levels and a monthly challenge.
  • Your journal stays yours. Back it up to a file, restore it on a new iPhone, or export your movies to Letterboxd.

When Letterboxd suits you better

Letterboxd is a social network for film lovers. Choose it if you want to write public reviews, follow friends, browse lists from the community, or use Android or a web browser.

Use both

Porcom exports the movies you’ve watched to a CSV file in Letterboxd’s import format, with the date you watched, your verdict and your notes: tap Profile › Data › Export as CSV, then import the file on Letterboxd. Series stay in Porcom, since Letterboxd doesn’t track them yet.
