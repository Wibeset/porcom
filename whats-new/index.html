What’s new in Porcom

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Every version of Porcom, the free movie and TV show journal for iPhone, newest first.

Version 1.2 — November 2026

  • Porcom en español. The whole app is now in Spanish too, with Latin American formats and titles from TMDB in Spanish.
  • A new look. The style of porcom.app: Headland One titles, an ink background with grain, dotted rules, two-tone icons and a coloured tab bar.
  • A more immersive Tonight. Swipe through full-screen posters of the series you’re watching, with the episodes and evenings left at your pace. Below: your rhythm, what’s up next, the month and your progress.
  • Statistics, redesigned. A goal ring that shows whether you’re on pace, a new goal sheet and a new streak card.
  • A new welcome in five steps, ending on your goal for the year.
  • When you finish a season, Porcom offers to add the next one.
  • Coutume, the makers’ upcoming habit app, in Profile.

Versions 1.1.1 to 1.1.3 — November 2026

  • Reliure and Arpente, the makers’ other apps, in Profile.
  • Shared images and their captions now point to porcom.app.
  • Minor fixes and improvements.

Version 1.1 — November 2026

  • What should I watch next? A wheel picks among your titles to watch, or among TMDB recommendations.
  • A wall of posters. Your library as a poster mosaic, finished titles grouped by year, and the seasons of a series or the movies of a saga stacked together, with their own page.
  • Rewatches. Watch a movie or a season again and Porcom records a new viewing.
  • An idea for tonight on the home screen when nothing is under way.
  • Holiday challenges. Halloween and Christmas challenges, plus secret milestones.
  • Watchlist sharing, as text or as an image.

First release — October 2026

Porcom arrives on the App Store, in English and French.
