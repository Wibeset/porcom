The best JustWatch alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

JustWatch is the go-to search engine for finding where a movie or series is streaming. But many people end up using it as a watchlist and an episode tracker too, and that’s where they start looking elsewhere: a guide full of ads, deals and, since October 2026, its own streaming service isn’t always the calmest place to keep track of what you’ve watched.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free journal for iPhone: a calm place to log the movies and series you watch, not a streaming search engine. You track each series episode by episode, keep a movie diary with dates, verdicts and rewatches, and save what you want to see in a watchlist, with a Spotted list for titles you caught in a trailer. To be clear, Porcom’s where-to-watch information comes from JustWatch itself, through TMDB, for Canadian subscription services. No account, no ads, and your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, adding up to hours on screen, streaks and a year in review.

Best for: keeping a private diary of everything you watch, on iPhone, without an account.

2. Trakt

Trakt tracks movies and series and can log them automatically by syncing with media servers like Plex and Jellyfin and with apps on many devices. Its where-to-watch information also comes from JustWatch. It needs an account and is free with ads, with a paid VIP plan. See our Trakt comparison.

Best for: automatic tracking across many devices and media servers.

3. Simkl

Simkl is an online tracker for TV shows, anime and movies, with a website and companion apps. It shows upcoming episodes and alerts you when new ones air, and can import lists from Trakt and AniList. It works with an account and is free, with optional paid plans. See our Simkl comparison.

Best for: anime fans and people who want an online history they can reach from any browser.

4. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the social network for film lovers: a diary, reviews, lists and friends, on iOS, Android and the web. It covers movies only for now; series are announced as in development. Its Pro plan adds streaming filters and alerts. See our Letterboxd comparison.

Best for: movie lovers who want to share reviews and follow friends.

5. Serializd

Serializd is a social network for TV: log episodes and seasons, write reviews and join discussions, on iOS and Android. It doesn’t track movies. See our Serializd comparison.

Best for: TV lovers who want a social feed around series.

When JustWatch suits you better

If your main question is “where can I watch this?”, JustWatch is the better tool. It covers far more than Porcom: free, ad-supported, rental and purchase options as well as subscriptions, price drops, new releases on each service, and many countries beyond Canada. It also works on Android, the web and Apple TV, and now streams some titles itself. Porcom shows only Canadian subscription services, on each title’s page, because it’s built for remembering what you watched rather than for searching.

Using both, or moving over

The two fit together well: search JustWatch to find where something is streaming, then log it in Porcom once you’ve watched it. Porcom doesn’t import from JustWatch or any other app, so you rebuild your lists by searching each title (data from TMDB) or typing it in by hand; a series is added one season at a time. From there, your journal stays portable: export a Porcom backup file to restore it on another iPhone, or export your watched movies as a CSV in Letterboxd’s import format under Profile › Data › Export as CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Is Porcom a streaming guide like JustWatch?

No. Porcom is a journal for the movies and series you watch. Each title’s page shows the Canadian subscription services where it’s streaming, but Porcom doesn’t search across rentals, purchases or other countries.

Where does Porcom get its streaming information?

From JustWatch, through TMDB. Porcom shows the Canadian subscription services for each title and checks them again each time you open the title’s page.

Can I import my JustWatch watchlist into Porcom?

No. Porcom has no importer. Add your titles by searching for them or typing them in by hand.

Does Porcom need an account?

No. Porcom is free, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, and we never receive it.
