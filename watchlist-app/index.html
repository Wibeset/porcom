A watchlist for movies and series, on your iPhone

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Keep every movie and series you want to see in one place, see where it’s streaming, and let Porcom pick tonight’s. Free, with no account and no ads.

Download on the App Store

To watch, and Spotted

Add a title to your To watch list in a tap. Saw a trailer for something that isn’t out yet? Put it in Spotted, and it waits there until its release.

Where it’s streaming in Canada

Each title’s page opens on its trailer and shows the subscription services where it’s available in Canada, from JustWatch data provided by TMDB.

What should I watch next?

Can’t decide? Spin the wheel and let chance pick among your titles to watch, or among TMDB recommendations.

Share your watchlist

Send your watchlist as text or as an image, for a movie night with friends.

Free and private

Porcom works without an account. Your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, which we can’t access.

Download Porcom on the App Store: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
