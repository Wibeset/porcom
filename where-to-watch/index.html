Where to watch it in Canada, on every title’s page

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Porcom shows where a movie or series is streaming in Canada, right next to its trailer and your verdict. Free for iPhone, with no account and no ads.

Download on the App Store

The streaming services, at a glance

Each title’s page shows the logos of the subscription services where it’s available in Canada, from JustWatch data provided by TMDB. Availability changes often, so Porcom checks it each time you open the page.

Pick the platform as you add

When you add a title, Porcom offers the services where it’s streaming, so you can note where you watched it.

Your hours by platform

Your statistics show how much time you spend on each platform, and on each genre.

Good to know

Porcom shows subscription services in Canada only, not rentals or purchases. Porcom is not affiliated with any streaming service, and its links are plain links.

Free and private

Porcom works without an account. Your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, which we can’t access.

Download Porcom on the App Store: free, with no account, no ads and no subscription.
