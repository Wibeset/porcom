The best BetaSeries alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

BetaSeries is a long-running French community for series fans: an episode calendar, comments on every episode, badges and a feed of what your friends watch. That is exactly why some people look elsewhere. Your history lives in an online account, the free version shows ads, going ad-free means joining the paid BetaSeries Club, and a lot of the app is built around the community. If you would rather keep a quiet, private journal of your series and movies, here are the alternatives worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV show journal for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no subscription. Your journal is stored on your iPhone and in your private iCloud; we never receive it. You track series episode by episode, one season at a time, keep a movie diary with dates, verdicts and rewatches, and build a watchlist that includes titles spotted in a trailer. Each title opens on its trailer and shows where it’s streaming in Canada, and your hours on screen add up to a year in review. The app is in English and French, and Spanish since version 1.2.

Best for: people who want a private, ad-free journal of series and movies on iPhone, without a community attached.

2. Simkl

Simkl tracks TV shows, anime and movies, on the web and in mobile apps, with a free account and optional paid plans. Its iPhone app can import from Trakt and AniList, and it alerts you when new episodes air. Like BetaSeries, it keeps your history in an online account. Porcom vs Simkl.

Best for: anime fans and people who want an online tracker that works across devices.

3. Trakt

Trakt tracks movies and series by episode, and can sync automatically with media centres such as Plex and Jellyfin. The free plan has ads and limits lists to 250 items; VIP costs US$59.99 a year. It runs on the web, iOS, Apple TV and more. Porcom vs Trakt.

Best for: people who watch through Plex, Jellyfin or Kodi and want their history logged automatically.

4. Serializd

Serializd is a social network for TV only: you log seasons and episodes, write reviews and follow friends. It is free on iOS and Android, with optional in-app purchases. It doesn’t track movies. Porcom vs Serializd.

Best for: series fans who enjoy the BetaSeries comment threads and want reviews and friends front and centre.

5. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the social network for film lovers, on iOS, Android and the web. It is free with ads, with Pro and Patron plans. It doesn’t track series yet, so it replaces only the movie side of BetaSeries. Porcom vs Letterboxd.

Best for: movie lovers who want public reviews, lists and a large community.

When BetaSeries suits you better

BetaSeries is a community, and that is its strength. Choose it if you like reading and writing comments under each episode, following what your friends watch, earning badges, or getting a notification when a new episode is out. It also runs on Android and in a web browser, its app is available in more languages, and it can import a TV Time archive you saved before TV Time shut down. Porcom does none of the social side, on purpose.

Switching from BetaSeries, or using both

Porcom has no importer, so moving over means adding what you’re watching now. Search for a title (the data comes from TMDB), pick the season you’re on and mark the episodes you’ve seen; a title TMDB doesn’t know can be typed in by hand. Most people start with the series under way and their watchlist rather than years of history.

You can also keep both: BetaSeries for the discussion, Porcom as your private journal. Once you’re in Porcom, your journal stays portable. Export a Porcom backup file and restore it on another iPhone, or export your watched movies as a CSV file in Letterboxd’s import format from Profile › Data › Export as CSV.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free BetaSeries alternative without ads?

Yes. Porcom is free on iPhone, with no ads, no subscription and no account. It tracks series episode by episode and movies in the same journal.

Can I import my BetaSeries history into Porcom?

No. Porcom has no importer. You add titles by searching TMDB or by typing them in, then mark the episodes you’ve already seen.

Does Porcom work on Android or the web?

No. Porcom is iPhone only. If you need Android or a browser, BetaSeries, Simkl, Trakt or Letterboxd are better fits.

Does Porcom have comments or friends like BetaSeries?

No. Porcom is a private journal with no social network. You can still share a title, your calendar, your year or your watchlist as an image or text.
