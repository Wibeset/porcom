The best Sofa Time alternatives in 2026

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sofa Time is a capable TV and movie tracker, and it can import TV Time’s data files, handy since TV Time shut down in July 2026. But it asks you to sign in for a profile, it has a social layer with comments, follows and an activity feed, and its Premium tier is sold by the month, the year or for life. If you’d rather keep a quiet, private journal with nothing to pay, here are the apps worth a look.

Download on the App Store

This guide is written by the makers of Porcom, so Porcom comes first. The other apps are listed honestly, with what they do better.

1. Porcom

Porcom is a free movie and TV show journal for iPhone, with no account, no ads and no subscription. You follow each series episode by episode, one season at a time, and keep a movie diary with the date, a verdict (👎 Meh, 👍 Liked, ❤️ Loved it! or 🔥 Devoured!) and every rewatch. Each title opens on its trailer and shows where it’s streaming in Canada; a watchlist holds what’s next, and your hours on screen add up to a year in review. Your journal stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

Best for: iPhone users who want a private journal for movies and series, without a profile or a Premium tier.

2. Trakt

Trakt is an online tracking service with apps on iOS, Apple TV, the web and more. It syncs automatically with media servers like Plex and Jellyfin, and imports history from IMDb, Letterboxd and TV Time. The free plan has in-house ads and caps lists at 250 items; VIP is US$59.99 a year. Sofa Time can sync with Trakt, so your history can follow you. Porcom vs. Trakt.

Best for: automatic tracking across many devices and media servers.

3. Simkl

Simkl tracks TV shows, anime and movies, on the web and in mobile apps, with a free tier and paid plans. It’s especially popular with anime fans. Like Trakt, it’s one of the two services Sofa Time syncs with. Porcom vs. Simkl.

Best for: anime alongside your shows and movies.

4. Serializd

Serializd is a social network for TV: you rate and review seasons and episodes, follow friends and join discussions, on iOS and Android. It’s free, with optional in-app purchases, and it doesn’t track movies. Porcom vs. Serializd.

Best for: talking about series with other viewers.

5. Letterboxd

Letterboxd is the social network for film lovers, on iOS, Android and the web, with reviews, lists and followers. It’s free with ads, with Pro and Patron plans, and TV series are announced but not available yet. Porcom vs. Letterboxd.

Best for: movie lovers who want public reviews and a community.

6. JustWatch

JustWatch is a free streaming guide on iOS, Android and the web: it tells you which services carry a title and lets you keep a watchlist. It’s built to find what to watch rather than to keep a detailed journal. Porcom vs. JustWatch.

Best for: finding where a title is streaming.

When Sofa Time suits you better

Sofa Time does a lot that Porcom doesn’t. Choose it if you use Android, an iPad or a Mac, if you want release reminders and a large streaming guide, or if you enjoy comments and following other viewers. It also imports backups from TV Time, IMDb and other trackers, and syncs with Trakt and Simkl, which Porcom can’t do.

Switching from Sofa Time, or using both

Porcom doesn’t import other apps’ files, so you rebuild your journal by hand, and it goes quickly:

  1. Download Porcom and search for the series you’re watching (data from TMDB), or type a title in if it’s missing.
  2. Pick the season, then the episode you’re on. Add the movies and seasons you’ve already seen as watched, with the date if you remember it.
  3. Keep a copy: Profile › Data › Export a backup saves a Porcom backup file you can restore on another iPhone, and Export as CSV sends your movies to Letterboxd.

Not ready to leave? Use both: keep Sofa Time for reminders and its community, and Porcom as your private journal, with verdicts, notes and your year on screen.

Questions

Can I import my Sofa Time library into Porcom?

No. Porcom has no importer: you add titles by searching (data from TMDB) or by typing them in, then mark the seasons and movies you’ve already seen as watched.

Is Porcom really free?

Yes. Porcom is free, with no ads, no subscription, no premium tier and no account.

Is Porcom on Android or the web?

No. Porcom is an iPhone app. If you need Android or a browser, Sofa Time, Trakt or Simkl will suit you better.

Where is my Porcom journal stored?

On your iPhone and in your private iCloud. We never receive it. You can also export a Porcom backup file and restore it on another iPhone.
